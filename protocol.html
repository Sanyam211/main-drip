01 // MANIFESTO

Water didn't change for a century. So we forked it.

Every can is a node. Charged with electrolytes, stripped of sugar, sealed in chrome. We don't ship inventory. We release drops. When a drop sells out, it's gone from the chain forever.

No restocks. No "back by popular demand." Scarcity isn't a marketing trick here — it's the architecture. Each serial number exists exactly once, printed on exactly one can, owned by exactly one person.

05 // FAQ
Is this actually water?
Yes. Sparkling electrolyte water — 355ml, zero sugar, zero caffeine, pH 7.4. The onchain part is the serial and supply ledger; the can itself is very much offchain and very much drinkable.
Do I need a crypto wallet to buy a can?
No. Email works fine for the allowlist and checkout. If you connect a wallet, your serial gets attributed to your address; otherwise we hold it in custody against your email.
What happens when a drop sells out?
The supply contract for that drop is frozen and the flavor recipe is retired. Sold-out drops never restock. Secondary trading of unopened cans is a community matter — we just guarantee no new supply.
How does shipping work?
Drops ship in cases of 6 or 12 within 14 days of mint close. Currently shipping to most regions; allowlist confirmation email includes the current shipping map for the active drop.
When is the next drop?
ULTRAVIOLET (Neon Berry, 10,000 cans) renders Q3 2026. Allowlist members get a 24-hour head start. After that — STATIC, date and flavor still encrypted.
